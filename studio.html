About us

A research team that also builds and films.

Why we exist

Proving something is true is one craft.

Moving people with it is another.

We hired for both.

Project Logos is fronted by three practitioners with complementary crafts — direction, method, and the camera — backed by a sixteen-person bench.

AJ
Principal 01 · Founder & Chief Executive

Ali Javed

"The person who scopes the work signs it off."

Economist and researcher (M.Phil., Economics, Jawaharlal Nehru University — thesis on health inequities across social groups in Uttar Pradesh). Founded Nous Network in 2022 and has served as Principal Investigator on national survey research, including a nationwide study of minority higher-education institutions and NFHS-based socioeconomic analysis. Leads client scoping and holds final sign-off across every engagement, has produced, presented and edited documentary and explainer films, and designed the AI-augmented operating architecture the studio’s production pipeline runs on.

AF
Principal 02 · Chief Researcher

Abid Faheem

"Every claim sourced, every method documented."

Public health researcher and social work professional (PhD & MPhil, Social Science in Health, JNU; MSW, Tata Institute of Social Sciences) with 8+ years across public health, education, maternal and child health, and nutrition — with organisations including Care India and a UNICEF-supported nutrition programme (Unnati Sansthan). Published in Social Science & Medicine and Sexual and Reproductive Health Matters. Has led large-scale surveys — including a 2,700-household socio-economic and school-education survey — multi-year M&E consultancies, and GIS-based spatial analysis in QGIS. Supervises the research bench — including AI-assisted qualitative coding with inter-coder reliability checks — and enforces the standard that makes the work defensible.

Principal 03 · Video Journalist & Director of Photography

Md Meharban

"Go where the story is."

Independent photojournalist, video journalist and drone pilot based in New Delhi, post-graduate of AJK MCRC, Jamia Millia Islamia. Seven years covering major news events across India, with work published in National Geographic, The New York Times, Reuters, the Associated Press, The Atlantic, Al Jazeera, The Caravan and Forbes. What we believe

Three commitments that shape how every engagement runs. The full list of six sits on our services page.

I

Methods before conclusions

We report what the evidence supports, including when it's inconvenient. A finding that flatters the client isn't worth more than one that doesn't.

II

Capacity, not dependency

Every engagement includes handover and documentation. We'd rather leave a capable team behind than a subscription.

III

One team, whole pipeline

The people who ran the fieldwork brief the people who cut the film — nothing gets lost in a handoff between agencies.

Behind the principals, a full bench

We draw on a 16-person specialist team — researchers, a senior journalist, editors, motion designers, a chief visual producer and outreach operators — organised in a documented eight-stage production pipeline from commissioning to impact reporting.
