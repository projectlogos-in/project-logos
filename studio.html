The Studio

A research team that also builds and films.

Project Logos exists because the two halves of evidence-based work — proving something is true, and getting anyone to act on it — are usually done by two different teams who don't talk to each other. We built one team that does both.

What we believe

Three commitments that shape how every engagement runs. The full list of six sits on our services page.

I

Methods before conclusions

We report what the evidence supports, including when it's inconvenient. A finding that flatters the client isn't worth more than one that doesn't.

II

Capacity, not dependency

Every engagement includes handover and documentation. We'd rather leave a capable team behind than a subscription.

III

One team, whole pipeline

The people who ran the fieldwork brief the people who cut the film — nothing gets lost in a handoff between agencies.

The principals

Three practitioners with complementary crafts: research, evaluation, and the camera.

Ali Javed

Founder & Chief Executive

Economist and researcher (M.Phil., Economics, Jawaharlal Nehru University — thesis on health inequities across social groups in Uttar Pradesh). Founded Nous Network in 2022 and has served as Principal Investigator on national survey research, including a nationwide study of minority higher-education institutions and NFHS-based socioeconomic analysis. Leads client scoping and holds final sign-off across every engagement, and has produced, presented and edited documentary and explainer films.

Abid Faheem

Chief Researcher

Public health researcher and social work professional (PhD & MPhil, Centre of Social Medicine & Community Health, JNU; MSW, Tata Institute of Social Sciences) with 8+ years in mixed-method research, health systems and socio-economic studies. Has led large-scale surveys — including a 2,700-household socio-economic and school-education survey — and delivered qualitative-methods training in MAXQDA for international research consultancies. Supervises the research bench and enforces the standard that makes the work defensible: every claim sourced, every method documented.

Md Meharban

Video Journalist & Director of Photography

Video journalist, photojournalist and drone pilot with 7 years' experience covering major news events across India — work published in National Geographic, The New York Times, The Atlantic, Al Jazeera, Business Insider and TRT World, and collected in his photobook Hum Dekhenge. A WaterAid India Fellow mentored by Pulitzer Prize–winning photojournalist Danish Siddiqui, he leads field cinematography and studio production across documentaries, campaigns and evidence-linked visual reporting.
