Services

Research, evaluation and storytelling — under one roof.

Most organisations working for social change face the same two gaps: they don't have the evidence they need, or they have it and can't move anyone with it. We close both. Project Logos brings rigorous research and evaluation together with documentary and design capability — so the same team that collects your data can also build the dashboard your board reads and the film your community shares.

Pillar A

Research & Evidence

The foundational layer — studies, surveys and qualitative work built to be published, defended and acted on.

A1

End-to-End Research

You bring the question. We handle everything from framing it properly to putting a finished report in your hands.

What's included
  • Scoping and problem definition; converting a broad concern into answerable research questions
  • Literature review, desk review, and secondary data synthesis
  • Research design — sampling strategy, methodology selection, mixed-methods architecture
  • Tool development — questionnaires, interview guides, FGD protocols, observation checklists
  • Ethics review support, consent architecture, and data protection planning
  • Primary data collection — enumerator recruitment and training, fieldwork supervision, quality control
  • Data cleaning, coding, and analysis (quantitative, qualitative, or mixed)
  • Report writing, peer review, and design-ready output
Deliverables: research design note · validated tools · cleaned datasets with codebook · analysis files · full report · executive summary · presentation deck
Ideal for: baseline and endline studies, formative research, situation analyses, needs assessments, policy research, investigative and thematic reports
A2

End-to-End Survey

Sample to statistics. We design the survey, run it, and hand back numbers you can defend.

What's included
  • Sampling design — frame construction, sample size calculation, power considerations, weighting plans
  • Questionnaire design, translation, and cognitive pre-testing
  • Digital instrument programming (KoboToolbox, ODK, SurveyCTO, Google Forms) with skip logic and validation
  • Field team recruitment, training, and deployment; CATI/CAPI/telephonic/online modes
  • Live field monitoring — back-checks, spot-checks, GPS and audio audits, high-frequency checks
  • Data cleaning, weighting, and estimation with confidence intervals
  • Tabulation plan, cross-tabs, and analytical write-up
Deliverables: sampling note · programmed instrument · field protocol · cleaned and weighted dataset · tabulation pack · survey report · methodology annexe
Ideal for: perception and attitude surveys, household surveys, beneficiary satisfaction studies, KAP studies, rapid polls, panel and tracking surveys
A3

Qualitative Research & Analysis

Interviews, testimony and field notes turned into structured, citable findings.

What's included
  • In-depth interviews, key informant interviews, focus group discussions, oral history collection
  • Ethnographic and participant observation studies
  • Transcription and translation (including Hindi, Urdu and Hindustani material)
  • Systematic coding using grounded theory, thematic analysis, framework analysis or content analysis
  • Codebook development, inter-coder reliability checks, analytical memoing
  • Software-supported analysis in NVivo, MAXQDA and ATLAS.ti
Deliverables: interview guides · transcripts · codebook · coded project file · analytical memos · thematic findings report
Ideal for: lived-experience research, community consultation, sensitive-topic research, case study series, participatory research
Pillar B

Monitoring, Evaluation & Learning

A measurement system your team will actually use — and the honest evaluation to go with it.

B1

MEAL Framework Design & Data Analysis

A measurement system your team will actually use — and the analysis to go with it.

What's included
  • Theory of Change and results framework development
  • Logframe design; indicator selection with clear definitions, means of verification and baselines
  • MEAL plans, data flow maps, and roles-and-responsibilities matrices
  • Data collection tools and routine monitoring formats
  • Accountability mechanisms — feedback and complaints systems, community scorecards, participation protocols
  • Learning agendas, reflection processes, and after-action review formats
  • Ongoing analysis of routine monitoring data and periodic performance reporting
Deliverables: Theory of Change · logframe / results framework · indicator reference sheets · MEAL plan · monitoring tools · learning agenda · periodic analysis reports
Ideal for: new programme setup, donor compliance requirements, organisations scaling from informal tracking to a system
B2

Impact Assessment & Evaluation

Did it work, for whom, and why? An honest answer, with the evidence to back it.

What's included
  • Evaluation design — process, outcome, impact, formative, summative, and developmental evaluation
  • Experimental and quasi-experimental designs (RCT, difference-in-differences, propensity score matching, regression discontinuity) where appropriate and feasible
  • Theory-based and participatory approaches — contribution analysis, outcome harvesting, Most Significant Change, QuIP
  • SROI and value-for-money assessment
  • OECD-DAC criteria evaluation (relevance, coherence, effectiveness, efficiency, impact, sustainability)
  • Stakeholder validation workshops and management response support
Deliverables: evaluation matrix and inception report · data collection tools · findings report with recommendations · validation workshop · dissemination brief
Ideal for: end-of-project evaluations, mid-term reviews, donor-mandated assessments, internal learning exercises
B3

Documentation Frameworks

A defensible, repeatable way to record what happened — built for organisations working on rights, harm and accountability.

What's included
  • Incident taxonomy and typology design; definitional boundaries and inclusion criteria
  • Standardised intake and verification protocols, evidence grading, and source hierarchies
  • Chain-of-custody, data security, and survivor-centred consent practices
  • Trauma-informed interviewing protocols and interviewer duty-of-care
  • Database schema design and quality assurance workflows
  • Training rollout and documentation team supervision
Deliverables: framework manual · intake and verification forms · verification tiers · database structure · training package
Ideal for: human rights organisations, monitoring bodies, legal aid groups, fact-finding missions

How we work

Four ways to engage, one repeatable process.

Engagement models
ProjectA defined scope with a fixed deliverable and timeline.
RetainerOngoing research, MEAL or content support at an agreed monthly commitment.
Embedded supportWe work inside your team for a fixed period, building capacity as we go.
AdvisoryShort, focused input on design, methodology, or strategy without full delivery.
Our process
  • ListenA free scoping conversation to understand the problem, not just the brief.
  • ProposeA written proposal with methodology, timeline, team, deliverables and costs.
  • DesignInception phase, tools, protocols, and sign-off before anything goes to field.
  • DeliverExecution with agreed check-ins and no surprises.
  • Hand overEverything transferred, documented, and explained. Your data stays yours.

Principles

Six commitments that hold regardless of the engagement.

Methods before conclusions

We report what the evidence supports, including when it's inconvenient.

Consent is not a form

Participants understand what they're joining, and can leave it.

Your data is yours

Full transfer of datasets, code and files at close-out. No lock-in.

Language matters

We work in English, Hindi, Urdu and Hindustani, and we don't treat translation as an afterthought.

Capacity, not dependency

Every engagement includes handover and documentation.
